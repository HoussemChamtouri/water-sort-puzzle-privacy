Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Water Sort Puzzle ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information Collection and Direct Data

We respect your privacy. Water Sort Puzzle does not require user registration or account creation. We do not directly request, collect, or store any personal data such as your name, physical address, or phone number.

2. Third-Party Services and Automated Data Collection

To provide high-quality gameplay, serve advertisements, and analyze app performance, our app integrates trusted third-party SDKs provided by Google. These third parties may automatically collect device information, including Advertising IDs (GAID), IP address, and system specifications.

Third-party services used in this app include:

  • Google Play Services: Used for core application framework functionalities. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob: Serves Banner and Rewarded Video advertisements. AdMob uses device identifiers to provide non-personalized or personalized advertising based on your privacy settings. AdMob Privacy & Data Info
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP): Manages privacy consents and regulatory requirements for users in the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, and California.

3. Local Device Storage

The app utilizes local device storage (such as SharedPreferences) strictly to store offline game progression, unlocked level data, sound settings, and virtual in-game coin balances locally on your physical device. This data is not transmitted to external servers.

4. GDPR & EEA Privacy Rights

If you are residing in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Through the Google UMP consent banner presented upon launching the game, you have full control to consent to, reject, or manage your personal ad preferences at any time.

5. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CalOPPA)

California residents have specific privacy rights regarding personal information collection and ad personalization choices under CCPA/CalOPPA. You may opt out of personalized advertising consent options provided through the in-game consent settings menu.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Water Sort Puzzle is designed for a general audience and is not intentionally directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 years of age. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can remove such data.

7. Security

We value your trust in playing our app. While no method of digital transmission or electronic storage is 100% secure, we utilize standard industry practices and rely on secure infrastructure provided by Google Services to safeguard technical identifiers.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or regulatory changes. You are advised to review this page occasionally for any updates. Changes are effective immediately upon posting on this page.
